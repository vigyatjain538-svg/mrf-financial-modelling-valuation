# Financial Modelling & Business Valuation of MRF Limited

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the comprehensive financial analysis and business valuation of **MRF Limited**, one of India's leading tyre manufacturers. The valuation combines Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and Relative Valuation methodologies using Microsoft Excel to assess the company's financial performance, future growth potential and intrinsic value.

## Objectives

- Analyse historical financial performance and key financial metrics.
- Develop five-year financial projections based on operating assumptions.
- Estimate intrinsic value using DCF and comparable company valuation.
- Evaluate valuation sensitivity to changes in key financial assumptions.
- Formulate an investment perspective based on valuation outcomes.

## Project Scope & Methodology

### 1. Historical Financial Analysis (FY2022–FY2026)
- Revenue and profitability analysis.
- EBITDA and operating margin assessment.
- Capital expenditure and working capital analysis.
- Evaluation of historical financial trends.

### 2. Financial Forecasting (FY2027–FY2031)
- Revenue growth projections.
- EBITDA margin assumptions.
- Depreciation and amortisation forecasts.
- Capital expenditure and working capital projections.
- Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF) forecasting.

### 3. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation
- FCFF-based valuation methodology.
- Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) calculation.
- Cost of Equity estimation using CAPM.
- Terminal value estimation using:
  - Gordon Growth Model
  - Exit Multiple Method

### 4. Relative Valuation
Comparable company analysis using four listed industry peers:
- Apollo Tyres
- CEAT
- JK Tyre
- Balkrishna Industries

Valuation multiples:
- Price-to-Earnings (P/E)
- EV/EBITDA
- EV/Sales
- Price-to-Book (P/B)

### 5. Sensitivity & Valuation Analysis
- WACC and terminal growth sensitivity analysis.
- Football Field valuation analysis.
- Valuation reconciliation across different methodologies.
- Investment recommendation based on valuation findings.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Financial modelling, forecasting, valuation calculations and sensitivity analysis.
- **Financial Methodologies:** DCF, FCFF, WACC, CAPM and Comparable Company Analysis.
- **Analytical Techniques:** Financial statement analysis, scenario evaluation and valuation modelling.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Practical understanding of corporate financial modelling.
- Application of DCF and relative valuation methodologies.
- Financial forecasting using historical performance and operating drivers.
- Understanding the relationship between discount rates, growth assumptions and intrinsic valuation.
- Developing investment perspectives through fundamental financial analysis.
